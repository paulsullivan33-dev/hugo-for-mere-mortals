The lab — one real Hugo, one fictional astronomy club
================================================================

The lab is Hugo 0.168.0, the extended
build, downloaded as the official
release tarball for linux-amd64 from
the project's GitHub releases and
verified against the project's own
checksums file before it ran (SHA-256
answer: OK; transcript t00). It ran on
the build machine: an Ubuntu Linux
sandbox, no display, Hugo called as
./bin/hugo from a work folder.

The site built on it is the Hilltop
Astronomy Club — invented whole: the
club, its hall, its members, and its
opinions about the Moon are fixtures
for any reader to reproduce, modelled
on no real club or person. Its full
source (content, hand-written layouts,
config, the lot) is kept at
hugo-lab/site/; its photograph is a
640x400 PNG the lab generated (a dark
field with stars) so the image pipeline
had something honest to resize.

What the lab did, in order:

  * Scaffolded with `hugo new site`
    and built immediately, on purpose,
    to record what an empty site does:
    warnings about missing layouts, and
    only XML outputs (t01, t02).
  * Wrote the config, four pieces of
    content plus a draft, and hand-made
    templates — first in the pre-0.146
    _default layout, because that is
    what older tutorials teach.
  * Found, by inspecting a tag page,
    that every term page was rendered
    by the taxonomy template and the
    term template never ran; proved it
    with --templateMetrics; moved the
    templates to the current layout
    (layouts/ root, _partials,
    _shortcodes) and proved the fix the
    same way (t90). The guide teaches
    the current layout throughout.
  * Built the finished site (27 pages,
    379 ms cold, 88 ms warm), and took
    the output apart: sitemap, RSS,
    shortcode results in the HTML, the
    Sass product with its variables
    resolved, the bundle image beside
    its 300px resized copy (t03, t04).
  * Served it and asked real questions
    with curl: all pages 200, an
    invented address 404, the draft
    present only under --buildDrafts
    (t05, t07).

Two deprecations were met on the way
and are kept in the transcripts rather
than smoothed over: .Site.LanguageCode
in an early template (fixed to
.Site.Language.Locale), and css.Sass's
libsass warning, which stands in the
final build and is discussed in
chapter 10 as the small migration it
announces.

Nothing was deployed anywhere: the lab
machine has no public address. Chapter
11 is the documented pattern, and the
lab notes and criteria.md both say so.

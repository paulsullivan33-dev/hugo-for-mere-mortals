---
title: 'Moonwatch evening: twelve of us, one flask short'
date: 2026-10-10
tags: ['moon', 'observing']
---

Twelve members turned out for the October
Moonwatch. Mare Imbrium was the evening's
favourite; two beginners found the Alpine
Valley unaided, which is a better result
than it sounds.

We logged the session in the club book and
argued, happily and without resolution,
about whether the straight wall is easier
at first quarter or last.

Half the club started exactly like this: a
pair of borrowed 10x50 binoculars, a garden
chair, and the Moon.

The trick nobody tells you is to brace
your elbows. Rest them on a fence or a car
roof and the Moon stops dancing. The
terminator — the line between light and
dark — is where the craters show their
shadows.

The club reflector gets a mirror wash
about once a year, and only because the
hall is dusty. Telescope mirrors work fine
with a surprising amount of dust on them;
every wash is a small risk, so we wash
rarely and gently.

Below is the mirror before its bath,
photographed under the hall lights. The
specks look worse than they are.

At opposition Jupiter is up all night and
as close as it gets this year. Through the
club's 8-inch Dobsonian all four Galilean
moons were strung out in a line by 21:30,
and the Great Red Spot crossed the middle
of the disc just before midnight.

{{< skynote >}}
If you missed it, the planet stays well
placed for another month. Opposition is
the best night, not the only night.
{{< /skynote >}}
